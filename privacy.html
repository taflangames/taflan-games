Fish Months – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Fish Months ("the game") is a single-player game for Android. This policy explains what information the game and its advertising partner handle. Developer / contact: Taflan Games, gamerdede2026@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, e-mail address, location or contacts. We do not run our own servers for the game.

  • Game progress (levels, coins, records, language, settings) is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded video ads, only when you choose to watch one. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) the game shows a consent form so you can choose how your data is used for ads. You can reset or disable the advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). More information: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. The only third party that receives data through the game is Google (AdMob), as described above.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, delete or restrict the use of your data. Because we do not hold personal data about you, requests about ad data should be directed to Google; for anything else, contact us at the e-mail above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
