Fish Months

A cozy fishing game for Android

Swing the rod, tap at the right moment and hit a coin goal in a new seaside scene every month, for twelve months. Chase combos, dodge rocks and sharks, collect 18 fish for your Fish Book and dress up Coral, Mochi and Biscuit. Each round lasts 60 seconds. No account needed, play offline, available in 14 languages.
