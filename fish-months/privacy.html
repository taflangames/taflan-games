Fish Months – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Fish Months ("the game") is a single-player game for Android and iOS published by Taflan Games. This policy explains what information the game and its advertising partner handle. Contact: gamerdede2026@gmail.com.

Information we collect

The game has no accounts. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, location, contacts or photos, and we do not run our own servers for the game.

  • Game progress (months cleared, coins, records, unlocked items, language and sound settings) is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.
  • Sharing a picture. When you tap Share, the game creates an image of your calendar or result on your device and passes it to your device's share menu (Android share menu or iOS share sheet). You choose where it goes. The image is not sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded video ads, only when you choose to watch one (for example to get extra seconds or to double the coins of a round). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS), IP address (used to estimate your general location), device and app information and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. On iOS, the game asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising identifier is used for tracking; if you decline, ads can still be shown but they will not be tracked across other companies' apps and websites. Where required, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the game shows a consent form so you can choose how your data is used for ads.

You can reset or disable your advertising ID in your device settings: on Android, Settings → Google → Ads; on iOS, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking (you can also change your choice for this game there). More information: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

Through the advertising SDK, Google may also receive information about the ads you see, how you interact with them, and crash and performance information about the game, which it uses to show and measure ads and to improve the SDK.

Sharing of data

We do not sell your personal data. The only third party that receives data through the game is Google (AdMob), as described above.

Children

Fish Months is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, delete or restrict the use of your data. We do not hold personal data about you, so requests about ad data should go to Google. For anything else, write to us at the e-mail address above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
